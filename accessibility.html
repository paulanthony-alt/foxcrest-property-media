Accessibility Statement

Effective [DATE]

Foxcrest Property Media wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website and find out about our services.

What we've done

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. So far we have:

  • Used text and button colors with enough contrast to read easily.
  • Made every part of the site usable with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines and a "skip to content" link.
  • Labelled every form field and given clear error messages.
  • Built the layout to work on phones, tablets and desktops, and when text is zoomed.
  • Respected your device's "reduce motion" setting.

Known limitations

Some walkthrough videos may not yet have captions or audio descriptions. We're working on adding them. If you need a description of any property video, or information in another format, just ask and we'll provide it.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, please email hello@foxcrestpropertymedia.com. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we'll do our best to fix it or give you the information another way, usually within 5 business days.
