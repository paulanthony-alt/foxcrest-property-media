Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE]

In short: we only collect what you type into our quote form, we use it to answer your request and do the job, and we never sell it. This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we are

Foxcrest Property Media ("we", "us") makes video walkthroughs of properties and runs property marketing campaigns. You can reach us about privacy at hello@foxcrestpropertymedia.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us through the quote form or by email or phone: your name, email address, phone number, your role (for example, agent or home owner), the property address, the services you're interested in, and anything you write in your message.
  • Booking and payment details if you hire us, such as invoices and payment confirmations. Card payments, if offered, are handled by a payment provider; we don't store full card numbers.
  • Footage of the property we film for you, as described in our Terms of Service.

We don't knowingly collect sensitive information, and we ask that you don't include any in your message.

How we use it

  • To reply to your quote request and give you a price.
  • To schedule, carry out and deliver the work you book.
  • To send invoices and keep business and tax records.
  • To contact you about your request or booking. We won't add you to a marketing mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with services that help us run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • Email and form services that deliver your quote request to us (for example, your email provider, or a form service such as Formspree if we use one).
  • Website hosting (GitHub Pages), which, like any web host, processes basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Video hosts (YouTube or Vimeo). Nothing loads from these services until you press play on a video. After that, they handle data under their own privacy policies. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and ask Vimeo not to track you.
  • Payment and accounting tools if you hire us.
  • Authorities, if we're required to by law.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't set advertising or analytics cookies, and our fonts are hosted on our own site rather than loaded from a third party. If we add privacy-friendly visitor statistics in future, we'll update this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests that don't turn into a booking for up to 12 months, then delete them. For clients, we keep booking and payment records for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes.

We don't sell or share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (targeted ads based on your activity across other sites). We don't use advertising trackers on this website. Because of this, there's nothing to opt out of, but if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) or similar opt-out signal, we treat it as a valid request not to sell or share your information. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy and add an opt-out link before it does.

Your choices and rights, including deletion

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop us contacting you.

Email a deletion request  or email hello@foxcrestpropertymedia.com with "Data request" in the subject.

Tell us the name and email you used so we can find your records. We'll confirm when it's done, usually within 30 days and always within the time the law requires. We may keep information we're legally required to keep (such as tax records for paid jobs), and we'll tell you if so. Depending on where you live (for example, California or other states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them. We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as using secure (HTTPS) connections and limiting who can access it. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
